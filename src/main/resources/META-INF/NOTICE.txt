AE MonitorNet (modid: ae_monitornet) - third party notices
==========================================================

This mod is a derivative work. The ME throughput monitor parts are ported from
AdvancedAE (its 1.20.1 line) down to Minecraft 1.19.2, and the same parts are
used by the 1.20.1 build. The "advanced" throughput monitor (its sampling
history, unit derivation, chart and the terminal UI) is our own implementation.

1. AdvancedAE  (https://github.com/pedroksl/AdvancedAE)
   Author: Pedroksl
   License: GNU LGPL-3.0
   Used for: the ME throughput monitor part (ThroughputMonitorPart), its
   sliding-window rate cache (ThroughputCache) and the per-tick sampling
   behaviour, which this mod ports from the 1.20.1 line down to 1.19.2. The
   rate unit cycling (per tick / second / minute / 10 minutes) and the "monitor
   a configured item/fluid and draw an icon + amount + rate on the part face"
   behaviour come from that implementation.

2. Applied Energistics 2  (https://github.com/AppliedEnergistics/Applied-Energistics-2)
   License: GNU LGPL-3.0
   Used as: required dependency and API surface (parts, grid services, menus,
   screens, widgets, storage watchers). No AE2 source file is copied into this
   mod; AE2 is compiled against, never bundled.
   Also: every part and item face is referenced straight out of AE2's own
   resources (ae2:part/..., ae2:item/...); none of AE2's textures is copied into
   this jar. The only files under assets/ae_monitornet/textures/ are this
   project's own art: the two terminal light layers it draws itself
   (part/terminal_front_medium.png, part/terminal_front_bright.png) and the
   configurator icon (item/throughput_monitor_configurator).

Original work in this mod (not derived from the projects above):
  - ThroughputHistory: discontinuity-aware sampling history, per-window series
    derivation (amount / rate / acceleration) with Tukey-fence trimming and
    bucket downsampling.
  - ThroughputChart: the in-GUI line chart renderer.
  - AdvancedThroughputMonitorPart, the storage monitor terminal (part + item),
    the draggable per-target chart windows, the block highlight ("locate")
    feature, the terminal row merging/sync protocol and all of this mod's own
    textures (the two part light layers and the configurator icon).
